About NovaLink

Integrating Sustainable Ecosystems with Global Digital Solutions.

NovaLink Global (Private) Limited is a Sri Lankan business platform focused on agriculture, livestock, trading, retail, digital transformation, software engineering and consultancy.

Our Business Model

We connect physical products, digital services and professional knowledge to create flexible opportunities for customers, suppliers and strategic partners.

We aim to develop commercially useful businesses while considering sustainability, resource efficiency and climate resilience.
